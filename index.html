🛡️ Individual Security Guidelines

Essential steps to secure your personal accounts and digital assets against modern cyber threats:

  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) across all critical accounts.
  • Avoid clicking suspicious links, unknown attachments, or phishing messages.
  • Use strong, unique passwords for every online service and platform.
  • Keep operating systems and security software updated regularly.

⚠️ Security Alerts & Status

High Alert Status: Multiple malicious campaigns targeting public infrastructure and user accounts through advanced social engineering have been detected and neutralized.


We advise maintaining extreme vigilance, declining requests from unverified sources, and never sharing authentication codes.
